title: "Test With Chrome Extension"
description: "See AdUnblock recover the ads on your site, under your own ad blocker, before you install the tag"
---

The AdUnblock DevTool extension shows AdUnblock recovering the ads on your own site, in your own browser, before you add the tag to your site. Only your browser sees the test: your visitors aren't affected.

<Info>
  There's nothing to copy or paste. The extension connects to your AdUnblock
  account through your dashboard.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get ready">
    - An AdUnblock account. [Sign up free](https://ad-unblock.com/signup) if you don't have one.
    - Chrome with an ad blocker installed and switched on for your site: AdBlock, Adblock Plus, uBlock Origin or AdGuard.

    AdUnblock only steps in when it finds an ad blocker. Without one, your ads show as they always do, and there is nothing to recover.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Install the extension">
    - Open [**AdUnblock DevTool**](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/adunblock-devtool/gnlaoijjmmehcmccdkmbbcmmlpegcpmi) in the Chrome Web Store and click **Add to Chrome**.
    - Pin it to your toolbar, so its icon is one click away.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your account">
    - Open your website in Chrome and click the **AdUnblock DevTool** icon.
    - Click **Connect your AdUnblock account**. Your dashboard opens in a new tab; sign in if you need to.
    - If your site isn't in your account yet, add it there. You don't need to install the tag first.

    The dashboard tab then closes on its own, you're back on your site, and the test starts. You only connect once: after that, click the icon on any of your sites and choose **Test ad recovery**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read the result">
    The page reloads with AdUnblock on, and the extension checks three things:

    ✅ **Recovery allowed**: your account can recover ads on this site

    ✅ **Ad blocker found**: your ad blocker is on for this site

    ✅ **Ads showing**: how many ads are on the page despite your ad blocker

    The number of ads also shows on the extension's icon. When all three pass, install the tag from your dashboard's setup guide to recover ads for every visitor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Before your tag is installed, test page views use your account's impressions,
  like any other recovered impression. The free plan includes 10,000 impressions
  a month.
</Note>

## If a check fails

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Your site isn't in this account">
    Add the site in your dashboard under **Manage Sites**, then click **Test again**. If you have more than one AdUnblock account, check that the extension uses the one that has this site.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Recovery is switched off for this site">
    Switch it on in **Manage Sites**, then test again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your account has used all its impressions">
    Upgrade your plan from your dashboard, then test again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No ad blocker found">
    Turn your ad blocker on for this site. A site you have allowlisted in your ad blocker counts as off.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No ads showing yet">
    Some sites load their ads as you scroll. Scroll down the page, then click **Count ads again**. Still none? Write to us with your site's address.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Testing a client's site

Agencies can test a site in a client's account without connecting: click **Enter an account ID instead** and type the client's 8-digit Account ID, shown at the top of their AdUnblock dashboard.

## Good to know

- The extension adds AdUnblock after your page starts loading, while an installed tag loads with the page. On sites with complex ad setups, results can differ a little from what your installed tag will do.
- The extension keeps only your Account ID and your sites' domains, in Chrome; your AdUnblock dashboard passes them to it. It works on a page only when you click its icon there. See the [privacy policy](https://ad-unblock.com/privacy/chrome-ext).
